# Nuru TV Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Nuru TV is a video player. You connect it to an IPTV subscription of your own. Nuru TV has no user accounts and does not run a server that receives your viewing activity.

## What stays on your device

Nuru TV stores the following on your device:

- your IPTV provider details and login, kept in your device's secure storage where available
- playlists, channel and film lists, and programme guide data
- favourites, watch history, resume positions, and settings
- temporary images and the first part of films you open, used to start playback faster

We do not receive this information. Removing a profile or uninstalling the app removes the app's data from the device, subject to how your operating system handles uninstalling.

## What we do not collect

Released versions of Nuru TV do not include advertising, analytics, or automatic crash reporting. We do not sell personal information.

## Services the app connects to

To work, the app connects directly from your device to the services below. Each one can see your IP address and the request it receives, and each has its own privacy practices.

| Service | When | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your IPTV provider | Loading your lists and playing video | Your login, what you open, and your IP address |
| The Movie Database (TMDB) | Showing film and series details | Titles or IDs being looked up |
| GitHub | Checking for updates, downloading updates, and loading some channel logos | The file requested |
| epgshare01 | Downloading the public programme guide, if used | The guide file requested |
| YouTube | Showing a trailer image, or opening a trailer | The trailer requested |
| Image hosts named in your provider's lists | Showing posters and logos | The image requested |
| Cloudflare or Quad9 | Only if you choose secure DNS in Settings | The server names being looked up |

## Reporting a problem

If you report a bug on GitHub, what you write is public. Do not include your IPTV login, stream addresses, or other private details.

## Children

Nuru TV is not directed at children under 13. Parents and guardians are responsible for the content available through a subscription.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date.
